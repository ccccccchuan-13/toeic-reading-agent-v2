辦公室公告

閱讀理解｜3 題。選擇答案後按「交卷」，即可查看分數與中文解析。

Office Announcement The Northbridge office will update its printing system on Tuesday, May 12. The printers on the second floor will be unavailable from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. During that time, employees may use the printer on the third floor. Employees who need to print large documents should do so before Tuesday. For questions, contact the IT team at help@northbridge.example. — Office Management

1. What is the purpose of the announcement?

｜正確答案 A

公告說明二樓印表機暫停使用的時段與替代地點。

2. Where can employees print during the update?

｜正確答案 C

公告寫明更新期間可使用三樓的印表機。

3. What are employees advised to do with large documents?

｜正確答案 B

第二段建議需要列印大型文件的員工在星期二之前列印。
