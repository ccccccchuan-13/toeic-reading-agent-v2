團隊工作坊通知

短文填空｜4 題。選擇答案後按「交卷」，即可查看分數與中文解析。

Subject: Customer Service Workshop Hello team, Our customer service workshop will be held on November 20 in Room 302. Please (1) ____ online by November 15 so we can prepare enough materials. The workshop will begin at 9:00 a.m. and (2) ____ at noon. There will be a short break at 10:30. (3) ____ If you cannot attend in person, please contact me for a video link. We look forward to your (4) ____. Best, Maya

1. (1)

｜正確答案 A

register 是報名，後文提到準備足夠教材，因此請大家線上報名。

2. (2)

｜正確答案 B

conclude 表示結束，與前面的 begin at 9:00 a.m. 對應。

3. (3)

｜正確答案 B

後一句提及不能親自出席可索取視訊連結，可知活動也提供線上參與。

4. (4)

｜正確答案 A

participation 是參與，符合期待同事參加工作坊的信末語意。
