DAY 05 · PART 5

職場詞彙練習

30 題單句填空，交卷後顯示分數及中文解析。

1. The assistant will _____ the documents before mailing them.

｜正確答案 A

organize documents 指整理文件。

2. The manager asked staff to _____ any technical problems immediately.

｜正確答案 B

report problems 指回報問題。

3. The new warehouse offers more _____ for storing finished goods.

｜正確答案 C

warehouse 提供較多 storage space。

4. Please read the _____ carefully before using the equipment.

｜正確答案 D

使用設備前應閱讀 instructions 操作說明。

5. The course is designed to _____ participants improve their writing.

｜正確答案 A

help someone improve 指協助某人改善。

6. The company has _____ its annual report on its website.

｜正確答案 B

post a report online 指將報告發布於網站。

7. The library will _____ its collection of travel books this year.

｜正確答案 C

expand a collection 指擴充館藏。

8. Please _____ your name and department on the form.

｜正確答案 D

enter information on a form 指在表格填入資料。

9. The applicant received an _____ to attend an interview.

｜正確答案 A

invitation to an interview 指面試邀請。

10. A technician is examining the machine to find the _____ of the problem.

｜正確答案 B

cause of the problem 指問題原因。

11. The products were placed on the top _____ of the display case.

｜正確答案 C

top shelf 指頂層架子。

12. The customer asked for a _____ because the item was defective.

｜正確答案 D

商品有瑕疵，顧客要求退款 refund。

13. Our team will _____ with the supplier on delivery dates.

｜正確答案 A

coordinate with 指與供應商協調。

14. Please _____ your phone during the performance.

｜正確答案 B

silence your phone 指將手機靜音。

15. The director will _____ the final decision by email.

｜正確答案 C

communicate a decision 指傳達決定。

16. The office offers a secure place to _____ bicycles.

｜正確答案 D

park bicycles 指停放腳踏車。

17. The new employee’s first _____ is to review the safety manual.

｜正確答案 A

first assignment 指第一項任務。

18. A full _____ is available on the event website.

｜正確答案 B

full schedule 指完整時程表。

19. Guests must _____ the registration desk before entering.

｜正確答案 C

visit the registration desk 指前往報到處。

20. The retailer has temporarily _____ its online payment service for maintenance.

｜正確答案 D

服務因維護暫時 suspended（暫停）。

21. The revised contract is attached for your _____.

｜正確答案 A

for your review 指供您審閱。

22. The caterer will _____ lunch for 40 participants.

｜正確答案 B

provide lunch for participants 指提供午餐。

23. The parcel is clearly _____ with the customer’s address.

｜正確答案 C

labeled with 指標示地址。

24. The financial statements are _____ on the company’s investor website.

｜正確答案 D

available on a website 指可在網站取得。

25. The manager has _____ the meeting until Thursday.

｜正確答案 A

postpone until Thursday 指延期至星期四。

26. Please store cleaning products in the designated _____.

｜正確答案 B

designated cabinet 指指定的儲物櫃。

27. A _____ will assist visitors in finding their seats.

｜正確答案 C

志工 volunteer 會協助引導入座。

28. The company will _____ a survey to learn about customer satisfaction.

｜正確答案 D

conduct a survey 指進行調查。

29. The restaurant advises customers to make _____ on busy weekends.

｜正確答案 A

make reservations 指事先訂位。

30. The training coordinator will _____ the list of participants tomorrow.

｜正確答案 B

finalize a list 指確定並完成名單。
