DAY 03 · PART 5

職場詞彙練習

30 題單句填空，交卷後顯示分數及中文解析。

1. The consultant will _____ a report on the proposed merger.

｜正確答案 A

prepare a report 指準備報告。

2. Please _____ the damaged item before returning it.

｜正確答案 B

photograph the damaged item 指將受損商品拍照。

3. The convention center offers free wireless Internet _____ to all visitors.

｜正確答案 C

Internet access 指網路使用權限。

4. The training course is _____ to new employees without charge.

｜正確答案 D

be available to 表示向特定對象提供。

5. Please _____ your membership before it expires at the end of June.

｜正確答案 A

renew a membership 指續訂會員資格。

6. The revised brochure contains the latest contact _____.

｜正確答案 B

contact details 指聯絡資訊。

7. The hotel will _____ guests when their rooms are ready.

｜正確答案 C

notify guests 指通知住客。

8. The conference organizer requires advance _____ for all workshops.

｜正確答案 D

advance registration 指事先報名。

9. The store can _____ an item that does not fit, provided it is unused.

｜正確答案 A

exchange an item 指更換商品。

10. The company will _____ its annual results in a public report.

｜正確答案 B

publish annual results 指公布年度成果。

11. Our new office has _____ storage space for all archived files.

｜正確答案 C

sufficient storage space 指足夠的儲存空間。

12. The manager asked for an _____ of the project costs before approving the budget.

｜正確答案 D

estimate of project costs 指專案成本估計。

13. Parking spaces are _____ on a first-come, first-served basis.

｜正確答案 A

停車位依先到先得原則分配，assigned 合適。

14. The store has _____ an additional cashier for the busy holiday season.

｜正確答案 B

hire an additional cashier 指增聘收銀員。

15. The shipment cannot leave until all documents have been _____.

｜正確答案 C

文件經 checked 核對後，貨物才可出運。

16. The café offers a _____ of sandwiches and salads at lunchtime.

｜正確答案 D

a selection of 指一系列可供選擇的品項。

17. Visitors should _____ the marked path while walking through the garden.

｜正確答案 A

follow the marked path 指沿標示步道行走。

18. The accountant noticed a _____ between the two totals.

｜正確答案 B

difference between two totals 指兩個總數之間的差異。

19. The theater will _____ ticket costs to customers whose performances were canceled.

｜正確答案 C

refund ticket costs 指退還票款。

20. The committee requires written _____ for all expenses over $500.

｜正確答案 D

written authorization 指書面授權。

21. Due to limited seating, early booking is strongly _____.

｜正確答案 A

座位有限，因此建議提前預訂。

22. The company will _____ staff with updated safety equipment.

｜正確答案 B

supply staff with equipment 指供應員工設備。

23. The museum has received a generous _____ from a local business.

｜正確答案 C

generous donation 指慷慨捐款。

24. Please make a _____ of the signed form for your records.

｜正確答案 D

make a copy 指製作副本。

25. The new system will _____ the time needed to process orders.

｜正確答案 A

reduce the time 指縮短所需時間。

26. The audience applauded at the end of the speaker’s _____.

｜正確答案 B

speaker’s presentation 指講者的簡報。

27. The order was returned because the mailing address was _____.

｜正確答案 C

郵寄地址錯誤 incorrect 導致訂單被退回。

28. The shop will _____ a free sample to the first 50 customers.

｜正確答案 D

offer a free sample 指提供免費試用品。

29. Please keep your desk clear during the office _____.

｜正確答案 A

辦公室整修 renovation 期間保持桌面淨空。

30. The librarian will _____ the requested books for three days.

｜正確答案 B

hold the books 表示保留書籍供讀者領取。
