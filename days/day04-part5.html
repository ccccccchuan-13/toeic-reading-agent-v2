DAY 04 · PART 5

職場詞彙練習

30 題單句填空，交卷後顯示分數及中文解析。

1. The project leader will _____ tasks among the team members.

｜正確答案 A

allocate tasks 指分配工作。

2. Applicants should include a telephone number where they can be _____.

｜正確答案 B

be reached 指可以聯絡到。

3. The accounting team will _____ the figures before issuing the report.

｜正確答案 C

verify figures 指核對數字。

4. The museum provides an audio _____ for visitors who prefer to explore independently.

｜正確答案 D

audio guide 指語音導覽。

5. The delivery driver will call when the order is ready to be _____.

｜正確答案 A

卸貨 unloaded 符合司機抵達配送地點的情境。

6. Please send the application to the address _____ on the form.

｜正確答案 B

listed on the form 指列在表格上的地址。

7. The workshop fee covers the cost of all instructional _____.

｜正確答案 C

instructional materials 指教學材料。

8. The publisher received _____ from readers about the new guide.

｜正確答案 D

receive feedback from readers 指收到讀者回饋。

9. The employee submitted a travel _____ to claim the hotel expense.

｜正確答案 A

travel receipt 指差旅收據，可用於申報住宿費。

10. A representative will _____ the proposal at tomorrow’s meeting.

｜正確答案 B

present a proposal 指提出或簡報提案。

11. The café offers a _____ for customers who bring reusable cups.

｜正確答案 C

discount 指折扣，符合自帶杯優惠情境。

12. Please _____ your files before installing the software update.

｜正確答案 D

back up files 指備份檔案。

13. The board plans to _____ the new policy in January.

｜正確答案 A

implement a policy 指實施政策。

14. The first-aid kit is _____ in the cabinet beside the elevator.

｜正確答案 B

be located in 指位於某處。

15. The product is no longer in production, but replacement parts are still _____.

｜正確答案 C

零件仍可取得 available。

16. The organizers will _____ a reminder to all registered participants.

｜正確答案 D

send a reminder 指發送提醒。

17. The printer requires regular _____ to operate reliably.

｜正確答案 A

regular maintenance 指定期保養。

18. The manager _____ the request because all required documents were complete.

｜正確答案 B

文件齊全，主管核准申請 approved。

19. The restaurant has added several vegetarian _____ to its menu.

｜正確答案 C

vegetarian options 指素食選項。

20. The training room is _____ with a screen and a projector.

｜正確答案 D

be equipped with 指配備。

21. The store will _____ unopened items within 14 days.

｜正確答案 A

accept unopened items 指接受未拆封商品。

22. The committee must _____ a date for the annual meeting.

｜正確答案 B

select a date 指選定日期。

23. The office manager ordered extra _____ for the copying machine.

｜正確答案 C

影印機需要補充紙張 paper。

24. The brochure is _____ at the reception desk in three languages.

｜正確答案 D

available at 指可在某處取得。

25. The supplier will _____ us of any changes to the shipping date.

｜正確答案 A

inform someone of changes 指告知變更。

26. The tour begins _____ at 9 a.m., so guests should arrive early.

｜正確答案 B

promptly at 9 a.m. 指準時九點開始。

27. Customers may _____ their reservation through the hotel website.

｜正確答案 C

modify a reservation 指修改預約。

28. The marketing department is collecting data to _____ customer preferences.

｜正確答案 D

identify preferences 指辨識偏好。

29. The package was _____ in protective material before it was shipped.

｜正確答案 A

wrapped in protective material 指以保護材料包裝。

30. The new employee will _____ an orientation session on Monday.

｜正確答案 B

attend a session 指參加說明會。
