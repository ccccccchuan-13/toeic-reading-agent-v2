DAY 01 · PART 5

職場情境單句填空

30 題詞彙與搭配詞。完成後按交卷，查看得分及解析。

1. Please review the contract before you ____ it.

｜正確答案 A

sign a contract 是簽署合約，其他選項分別是收集、租用、摺疊。

2. The museum will remain closed for building ____ through Friday.

｜正確答案 B

building repairs 指建築維修，符合閉館的原因。

3. To reserve a seat, please ____ the registration form by noon.

｜正確答案 C

submit the registration form 指提交報名表。

4. All staff members must wear an identification ____ inside the laboratory.

｜正確答案 D

identification badge 是識別證，符合進入實驗室的要求。

5. The marketing team will ____ a new advertising campaign next week.

｜正確答案 A

launch a campaign 指推出活動；句中 new 表示即將推出。

6. Please keep the receipt as ____ of your purchase.

｜正確答案 B

proof of purchase 指購買證明。

7. Due to heavy traffic, the delivery may be ____ by an hour.

｜正確答案 C

交通繁忙可能造成配送 delayed（延遲）。

8. We welcome customer ____ on how to improve our service.

｜正確答案 D

customer feedback 指顧客回饋。

9. The hotel offers a complimentary airport ____ for its guests.

｜正確答案 A

airport shuttle 是機場接駁車，符合旅館提供給住客的服務。

10. The seminar has reached its maximum ____, so registration is now closed.

｜正確答案 B

maximum capacity 指最高容納人數，額滿所以停止報名。

11. Please contact the front desk if you need to ____ your room reservation.

｜正確答案 C

modify a reservation 指修改預訂。

12. The finance department will ____ all travel expenses at the end of the month.

｜正確答案 D

reimburse expenses 指報銷費用。

13. The technician will ____ the air conditioner tomorrow morning.

｜正確答案 A

technician 將檢查空調，inspect 符合語意。

14. The job advertisement asks applicants to submit a résumé and a cover ____.

｜正確答案 B

cover letter 是求職信，與 résumé 並列。

15. The store is offering a 20 percent ____ on winter coats.

｜正確答案 C

discount on winter coats 指冬季外套折扣。

16. Our supplier notified us of a shortage of raw ____.

｜正確答案 D

raw materials 是原物料，符合供應商通知短缺的情境。

17. Please ____ the safety instructions before operating the machine.

｜正確答案 A

操作機器前應閱讀安全指示，read instructions 搭配自然。

18. The conference room can ____ up to fifty people.

｜正確答案 B

accommodate 表示可容納，與人數搭配。

19. The board will make a final ____ on the proposal next week.

｜正確答案 C

make a decision on the proposal 指對提案作決定。

20. A confirmation email will be sent once your payment is ____.

｜正確答案 D

付款經處理後寄確認信，processed 符合語意。

21. The applicant has five years of ____ in project management.

｜正確答案 A

experience in project management 指專案管理經驗。

22. The report includes a ____ of sales figures from the last quarter.

｜正確答案 B

summary of sales figures 指銷售數據摘要。

23. A temporary ____ will replace Ms. Lee while she is on leave.

｜正確答案 C

由臨時員工代替休假的李女士。

24. The company plans to ____ its operations into two new markets.

｜正確答案 D

expand operations into new markets 指拓展營運至新市場。

25. Customers should report damaged items within seven days of ____.

｜正確答案 A

收到配送商品後七天內回報損壞，delivery 符合語意。

26. The annual budget must receive the director’s ____ before it can be implemented.

｜正確答案 B

receive approval 指獲得核准，年度預算獲核准後實施。

27. Employees may ____ for the training course through the company website.

｜正確答案 C

register for a course 指報名課程。

28. The library has extended its opening ____ during the examination period.

｜正確答案 D

opening hours 指開放時間，extend 表示延長。

29. Our representative will ____ your question within two business days.

｜正確答案 A

answer your question 指回答問題。

30. The shipment was sent to the wrong ____, so it must be redirected.

｜正確答案 B

寄到錯誤地址才需要重新轉送。
