DAY 02 · PART 5

職場詞彙練習

30 題單句填空，交卷後顯示分數及中文解析。

1. Please return the signed agreement in the enclosed _____.

｜正確答案 A

enclosed envelope 是隨信附上的信封。

2. The guide will _____ visitors through the exhibition at 11 a.m.

｜正確答案 B

lead visitors through the exhibition 指帶領訪客參觀展覽。

3. A delivery _____ is available in the lobby for packages received after hours.

｜正確答案 C

delivery locker 是包裹櫃，供非營業時間收件。

4. The board postponed its vote until it received further _____.

｜正確答案 D

進行投票前需取得更多資訊 information。

5. All payments must be made in local _____.

｜正確答案 A

local currency 指當地貨幣。

6. Please _____ your appointment if you can no longer attend.

｜正確答案 B

不能出席時請取消預約，cancel an appointment。

7. A broken water pipe caused the temporary _____ of the swimming pool.

｜正確答案 C

水管破損導致泳池暫時關閉 closure。

8. The caterer can prepare vegetarian meals upon _____.

｜正確答案 D

upon request 指依要求提供。

9. The company _____ safety glasses to everyone entering the workshop.

｜正確答案 A

provide safety glasses to someone 指提供護目鏡。

10. Customers may _____ their tickets online or buy them at the door.

｜正確答案 B

purchase tickets 指購買門票。

11. The repair shop will provide a cost _____ before starting work.

｜正確答案 C

cost estimate 是成本估價。

12. The director will _____ the winners at the awards ceremony.

｜正確答案 D

announce the winners 指宣布得獎者。

13. The office is within walking _____ of the central station.

｜正確答案 A

within walking distance 是步行可達的距離。

14. The supervisor praised the team for meeting the project _____.

｜正確答案 B

meet a deadline 指如期完成。

15. Our website contains a detailed _____ of the return policy.

｜正確答案 C

detailed explanation of a policy 指政策詳細說明。

16. The bookstore will _____ its opening hours during the holiday season.

｜正確答案 D

extend opening hours 指延長營業時間。

17. To improve air quality, the city will plant more trees along major _____.

｜正確答案 A

道路沿線種樹，roads 符合空間語意。

18. The printer is out of paper. Please _____ the empty tray.

｜正確答案 B

refill the tray 指補滿紙匣。

19. New employees receive a staff _____ to access the building.

｜正確答案 C

staff pass 指員工通行證。

20. The hotel can _____ a taxi for guests departing early.

｜正確答案 D

arrange a taxi 指安排計程車。

21. Please place fragile items in separate boxes to prevent _____.

｜正確答案 A

分開包裝易碎物以防 damage（損壞）。

22. The committee will _____ proposals from three design firms.

｜正確答案 B

evaluate proposals 指評估提案。

23. The event was moved indoors because of the weather _____.

｜正確答案 C

weather forecast 是天氣預報。

24. Our technician will call to _____ the exact arrival time.

｜正確答案 D

confirm the arrival time 指確認抵達時間。

25. The museum asks visitors not to _____ photographs in this gallery.

｜正確答案 A

take photographs 是拍照的固定搭配。

26. Employees are encouraged to share their ideas during the brainstorming _____.

｜正確答案 B

brainstorming session 指腦力激盪會議。

27. The factory halted production while the faulty machine was under _____.

｜正確答案 C

under repair 指正在維修。

28. The committee selected a new logo after reviewing several _____.

｜正確答案 D

審閱數個設計 designs 後選出新標誌。

29. Visitors must obtain a parking _____ from the reception desk.

｜正確答案 A

parking permit 是停車許可證。

30. The publisher will _____ the revised guide next month.

｜正確答案 B

release the revised guide 指推出修訂版指南。
