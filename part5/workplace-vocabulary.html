PART5 · 練習

職場單字與搭配詞

單句填空｜3 題。選擇答案後按「交卷」，即可查看分數與中文解析。

1. Please submit your expense report before the monthly ____.

｜正確答案 A

deadline 指截止期限，符合在每月期限前提交報告的語意。entrance 是入口，discount 是折扣，branch 是分公司。

2. The meeting has been ____ until Friday because several staff members are away.

｜正確答案 B

postponed 表示延期。因數位員工不在，會議延到星期五。delivered 是送達，purchased 是購買，printed 是列印。

3. Customers can contact our support team if they need further ____ with the software.

｜正確答案 A

assistance 表示協助，搭配 need further assistance。luggage 是行李，traffic 是交通，scenery 是景色。
